The Engine

How I
Move

Content sets everything in motion
Content is the big gear. Every landing page, blog cluster, video script, and brief I write creates the surface area that growth and product work off. Without it, the other two gears have nothing to turn.
Content flows into Growth
SEO content drives organic signups. Landing pages feed paid campaigns. Lifecycle emails are content too — they determine whether someone activates or drops off. Content IS the growth channel.
Content powers Product
Positioning, messaging, battle cards, launch pages — all content. The clarity I built writing about products for 2 years is exactly what made me effective at product marketing.

Turn the big gear and the rest follow. That's how I'm built.
